### Two open questions from the design phase that didn't make it into validation.md

While re-reading design.md §7 for the spin-off prompt, I noticed two questions that are present in the design but not yet tracked as validations:

1. **Reaper for orphan supervisor PID files**: if the supervisor crashes hard (SIGKILL) it leaves a stale `supervisor.pid`. `run reattach` covers the explicit case, but if a supervisor crashes and is never reattached, `run list` shows it as `running` forever. Should there be a `run gc` command, or should `run list` itself probe `kill(pid, 0)` and downgrade stale runs to `crashed`?
2. **Event-log rotation policy**: design.md §1 says events.jsonl is canonical and replayable. There's no rotation policy. At ~1000 events/sec sustained (V4 numbers) a long-running orchestrate session could produce 100MB+ logs. Out of MVP scope, but worth a one-liner in `validation.md` so we don't ship without acknowledging it.

These don't block anything right now. Drop me a yes/no when you return and I'll either spawn a spike issue or just note them in design.md §9.
